Keyboard Shortcuts

The shortcuts work when the preview has keyboard focus. Click the preview first. The list is also shown in the window under the ? button.

Thumbnailer​

ShortcutAction
CCapture a thumbnail
FFrame the current asset
RMove the camera to the default view
0–9Move the camera to a camera bookmark
Ctrl + 0–9Save the current view to a camera bookmark

Viewport controls​

InputAction
Right mouse button + dragRotate the camera
Right mouse button + W A S DMove and rotate the camera
Right mouse button + mouse wheelChange the camera speed
Alt + left mouse buttonOrbit around the asset

The other editor viewport controls work too, for example Q and E to move down and up while holding the right mouse button.
